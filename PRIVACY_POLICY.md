# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 5, 2026**

## Introduction

PosLe (Point of Sale) ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the PosLe App. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

PosLe is a **local, offline-first point of sale application**. All your business data (products, transactions, inventory, customer information) is stored locally on your device. We do **not** collect or transmit any personal or business data from your use of the App.

### Automatically Collected Information

Since PosLe operates offline and does not connect to external servers, we do not collect:
- Usage analytics
- Device information
- Location data
- Personal identifiers
- Transaction history or inventory data

### Firebase Crashlytics

The PosLe App uses Firebase Crashlytics to help us diagnose and fix app issues. Crashlytics collects:
- Crash reports and stack traces
- Device model and OS version
- App version information
- Anonymous crash metadata

**Important:** Crashlytics data is anonymized and does **not** include:
- Your personal or business data
- Transaction details
- Product information
- Any personally identifiable information (PII)

Firebase Crashlytics collects data only when crashes occur and sends anonymized reports to Google's servers. You can find Google's privacy policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## How We Use Information

We use the limited information collected through Crashlytics solely for:
- Diagnosing app crashes and bugs
- Improving app stability and performance
- Fixing technical issues in future updates

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal or business data with third parties. The only data that may be transmitted externally is:
- **Anonymized crash reports** sent to Google via Firebase Crashlytics (as described above)

## Data Security

Since all your business data is stored locally on your device:
- Transaction records, product catalogs, and inventory data remain on your device
- We recommend using Android's built-in backup features if you wish to back up your data
- You maintain full control over your data

## Children's Privacy

The PosLe App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information.

## International Users

If you are accessing the PosLe App from outside your home country, please note that your information (including crash reports) may be transferred to and maintained on computers located outside your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction where the data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
